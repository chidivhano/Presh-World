Building Futures.

Preshworld is a dual-purpose culinary enterprise located in the heart of Protea Glen, Soweto. We operate both as an Accredited Culinary Academy and an Exquisite Bakery & Confectionery.

"Passion in the kitchen. Excellence in everything we do."

We believe that baking is both an art and a science. Our bakery delivers top-tier confections made with love and precision. Meanwhile, our academy is dedicated to providing accredited, high-quality education to aspiring bakers and chefs. From complete beginners to advanced students wanting to start their own bakery businesses, Preshworld is the beginning of something sweet, inspiring, and extraordinary.
